Information we collect

The contact form collects your name, phone number, project message and optional email address. We use these details to respond to your enquiry. Name, phone and message are required to submit the form. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Submissions go through Web3Forms to the recipient configured for the business’s account. Web3Forms states that submissions may be kept for up to three years and processed in multiple regions, including India. Read its privacy policy and data processing agreement for current details.
