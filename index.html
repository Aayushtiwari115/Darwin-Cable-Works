Quick answers to common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you service?

Darwin Cable Works services Darwin and surrounding areas in the Northern Territory. Contact us to check whether we can assist at your location.

Do you work with homes and businesses?

Yes. We work with residential and commercial customers in Darwin, with services including data cabling, Wi-Fi installation and network connectivity. Tell us a little about your property and project when you get in touch.

How do I request a quote?

Send an enquiry with a brief description of the work you need and your contact details, or call 0415 244 056. We can discuss your cabling, Wi-Fi or networking requirements and the next step.

What services does Darwin Cable Works provide?

Darwin Cable Works provides data cabling, structured cabling, data point installations, networking, antenna services, Wi-Fi router setup, wireless access point installation, network switches and POS system connectivity across Darwin and the Northern Territory. We also offer basic IT setup, troubleshooting and technical support for residential, commercial and business customers. Contact us to discuss a practical solution for your needs.

Can you help with Wi-Fi coverage?

We provide Wi-Fi installation and can discuss connectivity concerns such as areas with a weak signal. The suitable setup depends on the property and existing network equipment, so share those details when you contact Darwin Cable Works.

Do you provide POS connectivity?

Yes. POS connectivity is one of our listed services. If your point-of-sale equipment needs a network connection or is having connectivity trouble, contact us with a brief description of your setup and the issue.

Can you help if my docket system is not working?

We can help investigate network or cabling connectivity problems affecting a docket system. When you get in touch, let us know what is not working and whether other devices on the network are affected. If the issue is with the system's software or provider, you may also need to contact that provider. Send an enquiry or call 0415 244 056.

Can you help if my Wi-Fi access point (AP) is not working?

We can discuss Wi-Fi installation and connectivity issues involving an access point (AP). Tell us whether the AP has power, which devices are affected and when the problem began; this helps explain the issue. Send Darwin Cable Works an enquiry or call 0415 244 056 to discuss support in Darwin and surrounding areas.
